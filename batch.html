Honest limitations

  • Participation is public. Submission events reveal your address and direction. Zero-value and overflowing amounts are refunded, but the round threshold counts distinct submitting addresses and is not an anonymity guarantee.
  • Totals become public at close. makePubliclyDecryptable is intentional: the protocol needs the aggregate to size one AMM trade. Individuals stay hidden inside the aggregate.
  • Surplus side is public after settle. If everyone swaps the same direction, the net trade is visible. Netting is what shrinks it.
  • Settlement timing has MEV risk. The operator sees public totals and currently submits through the public MetaMask RPC. Flashbots/private transaction submission is not wired into this app.
  • Demo tokens are 1:1. vUSD/vETH trade at parity in the seeded pool, so swap output ≈ net. Real price impact is out of demo scope.

Deploys c-vUSD, c-vETH (ERC-7984 wrappers) and the VeilBatchSwap rounder through your MetaMask. The batcher swaps the net residual through Uniswap v4 (Universal Router + Permit2) against the demo pool.
